The Firm

Realize your constitutional right with qualified help

Lex Ardent LLP, originally established in Jaipur, India, has a history rooted in dedicated legal service. The firm was formally established in 2020 by advocates Akhilesh Pareek and Hardik Mishra. In 2025, the law firm successfully transitioned into a company.

Lex Ardent currently operates as a company, providing legal services to its clients, rendering legal advice, and its associates regularly appearing as counsel before the Hon’ble Rajasthan High Court, National Company Law Tribunal, Commercial Courts, National and State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions, Family Court, RERA, DRT and other tribunals and forums.
