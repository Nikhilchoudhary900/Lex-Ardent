Banking & Finance

Do you handle banking and finance matters?

Yes, we provide legal services for banking and finance matters including loan recovery, DRT/DRAT proceedings, financial disputes, and regulatory compliance. We represent banks, financial institutions, and borrowers.

What is DRT/DRAT and do you handle such cases?

DRT (Debt Recovery Tribunal) and DRAT (Debt Recovery Appellate Tribunal) handle recovery cases for banks and financial institutions. Yes, we have extensive experience representing both lenders and borrowers in DRT/DRAT proceedings.

Can you help with loan restructuring and settlement?

Yes, we assist with loan restructuring, one-time settlement negotiations, and settlement documentation. We help clients reach favorable settlements with banks and financial institutions.

Civil Litigation

What types of civil cases do you handle?

We handle various civil litigation matters including property disputes, contract breaches, recovery suits, specific performance, injunctions, and civil appeals. We represent clients in Civil Courts, High Courts, and the Supreme Court.

How long does a civil case typically take?

Civil cases can take anywhere from a few months to several years depending on the complexity, court backlog, and whether appeals are involved. We work efficiently to expedite proceedings while ensuring thorough representation.

Do you handle property disputes?

Yes, we specialize in property disputes including title disputes, partition suits, eviction matters, and real estate transactions. We represent clients in Civil Courts and RERA/REAT forums.

Can you help with contract disputes?

Absolutely. We handle breach of contract cases, specific performance suits, and commercial disputes. We represent clients in Commercial Courts and Civil Courts across India.

Consumer Law

Do you handle consumer forum cases?

Yes, we represent consumers in Consumer Forums (District, State, and National levels) for various consumer disputes including defective products, deficient services, unfair trade practices, and insurance claims.

What types of consumer complaints do you handle?

We handle consumer complaints related to defective products, deficient services, medical negligence, insurance disputes, banking services, e-commerce issues, and unfair trade practices.

How long does a consumer case take?

Consumer cases are generally resolved faster than regular civil cases. District Forum cases typically take 3-6 months, while State and National Commission cases may take longer depending on complexity.

Family Law

We handle a wide range of family law matters including divorce, child custody, maintenance, property disputes, adoption, and domestic violence cases. We represent clients in Family Courts across India.

How do you handle child custody cases?
