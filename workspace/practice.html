Practice Areas

At Lex Ardent, we offer a broad range of legal services designed to meet the needs of individuals, families, and businesses.

High Court Matters

Representation before High Courts in writ petitions, appeals, revisions, and complex constitutional, civil, and criminal matters.

Real Estate and RERA

Counsel for property transactions, RERA registration and compliance, builder-buyer disputes, and representation before RERA and REAT.

Consumer Disputes

Representation before District, State, and National Consumer Commissions for defective goods, deficient services, and unfair trade practices.

Cheque Bounce Cases

Filing and defending NI Act Section 138 matters, including demand notices, compounding, trial strategy, and recovery of dues.

Family Matters

Divorce, custody, maintenance, and matrimonial disputes handled with discretion, including mediation and family court representation.

Commercial Disputes

Strategic counsel for contract breaches, partnership conflicts, and commercial court proceedings for businesses and individuals.

Civil Litigation

End-to-end civil suits, injunctions, recovery actions, and appellate work before trial courts and higher forums.

Arbitration and ADR

Arbitration, mediation, and alternate dispute resolution for efficient, confidential settlement of commercial and civil disputes.

Motor Accident Claims

MACT claims for injury, disability, and compensation, with focused representation for accident victims and their families.

Succession and Inheritance

Wills, probate, mutation, and succession disputes to protect family property and inheritance rights.

Service Matters

Employment, departmental inquiries, promotions, and service litigation for employees, government servants, and institutions.

Contract Drafting

Drafting and vetting agreements, commercial contracts, and legal documents tailored to your transaction and risk profile.
